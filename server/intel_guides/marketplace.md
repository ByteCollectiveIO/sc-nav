## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Any member with something to sell, something to find, or a craft to order. |
| **What** | An org-only trading board for sales, auctions, barters, buy orders and craft requests. |
| **Where** | Launcher group Run the Org, route `#/market`. No watcher needed. |
| **When** | Whenever you'd otherwise post "WTS" or "WTB" in chat. |
| **Why** | Listings stay findable, and both sides confirm when a deal is done. |

## The basics

Everything is priced in aUEC, the in-game currency. Never real money.

The board has tabs for each listing type: **All**, **Sales**, **Auctions**, **Barter**, **Requests** (craft commissions) and **Buying** (buy orders). **My activity** shows the listings you've bid, offered or quoted on, plus deals waiting on your confirm. **My listings** shows your own posts. A search box, sort menu and filters sit above the list.

The app never moves goods or aUEC. Once a buyer, winning bidder or crafter is locked in, you meet in-game and trade. Then both of you confirm here. This dual confirm is the only way a deal completes, and each completed deal adds to both members' deal count.

## Buy something

Do this when you see a sale you want.

1. Open the **Sales** tab, or search for the item.
2. Click the listing to see the price, quantity, quality and pickup location.
3. Click **Buy now**. The listing moves to pending with you as the buyer.
4. Contact the seller using the in-game handle shown on the listing and meet up.
5. Trade the item for the aUEC in-game.
6. Come back to the listing and click **Confirm handoff**. The deal completes once the seller confirms too.

On an auction, enter an amount at or above the minimum and click **Place bid** instead. The highest bid at the end time wins.

## List an item for sale or auction

Do this when you have something to sell.

1. Click **+ New listing**.
2. Search the catalog for the item and pick it from the suggestions, then enter the quantity.
3. Choose a **Listing type**, such as a fixed-price sale or a timed auction.
4. Set the price for the whole quantity. For an auction, set a starting bid, an end date and an optional buyout.
5. Set **Availability** and a **Pickup / handoff location** so buyers know when and where.
6. Click **Post listing**.
7. When someone buys or wins, trade in-game, then click **Confirm handoff**.

If you logged the stock in Resource Manager, click **Sell** on the holding to open this form with quantity, quality and location filled in.

## Post a buy order

Do this when you can't find a seller.

1. Click **+ New listing** and pick the item and quantity.
2. Set **Listing type** to the buy order option.
3. Enter what you're **Paying (aUEC)** for the whole quantity.
4. Click **Post listing**. If your org has Discord notifications set up, members whose Resource Manager holdings carry the item get pinged.
5. Sellers reply with an offer. Review them on the listing and click **Accept** on the one you want.
6. Meet in-game, trade, and both click **Confirm handoff**.

## Commission a crafter

Do this when you want an item made to a spec.

1. Click **+ New listing** and set **Listing type** to the craft request option.
2. Search for the blueprint to craft. The form shows its materials list.
3. Choose who supplies the **Materials**.
4. Move the quality sliders to set the minimum quality you want for each material. The form estimates the resulting stats.
5. Add an optional **Budget (aUEC)** and a needed-by date, then click **Post listing**.
6. Crafters send quotes. Accept the one you want, and the job moves to pending.
7. When the crafted item is handed over in-game, both of you click **Confirm handoff**.

To pick a specific crafter, open the **Requests** tab, find their storefront and click **Request a craft**. Only that crafter can quote it.

## Tips

- Both sides must click **Confirm handoff**. If your trade partner goes quiet after the handoff, remind them.
- Price fields are for the whole quantity, not per unit.
- If you craft, open the **Requests** tab and tick **Requests I can craft** to see jobs that match your blueprint library.
- A listing left untouched for a while gets a stale badge. Click **Still available** on it to clear the badge.
- The seller can click **Cancel listing** on an open or pending listing.
