## {orange}At a glance{/orange}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Any member who gathers, stores, crafts or contributes materials. |
| **What** | A shared ledger of org goals, member holdings and blueprint libraries. |
| **Where** | Launcher group Run the Org, routes `#/goals`, `#/inventory` and `#/blueprints`. No watcher needed. |
| **When** | After a mining or hauling session, or when your org posts a goal. |
| **Why** | The game has no shared org storage, so this is how the org knows who has what. |

## {orange}The basics{/orange}

Resource Manager has three tabs that share one item catalog.

- **Goals** are targets for the org or for you alone: materials to gather, blueprints to get unlocked across members, or evidence to log in a named survey area.
- **Inventory** lists your holdings. Each row is one lot: an item, a place and a quality.
- **Blueprints** is your library of craft recipes, checked against your free stock.

Quality is the game's 0 to 1000 grade on a lot. Ten SCU (standard cargo units) of Q800 Iron and ten of Q300 at the same station are two rows. Leave quality blank if you don't know it. Q0 means station-bought, not a low grade.

A contribution to a goal is a pledge, not a transfer. The stock stays with you, and you can withdraw it later.

## {orange}Log what you hold{/orange}

Do this after a session so goals and the org rollup stay accurate.

1. Open the **Inventory** tab. It starts on **My holdings**.
2. Type the item and pick it from the suggestions.
3. Enter the amount and, if it applies, the quality the game shows.
4. Type a location and pick it from the list.
5. Click **Log holding**. Logging the same item, place and quality again adds to that lot.
6. Use **Edit** on a row to set an exact amount, or **Split** to move part of a lot to another quality or place.

## {orange}Contribute to an org goal{/orange}

Do this when your org posts something it needs gathered.

1. Open the **Goals** tab and click the goal.
2. Under **Log a contribution**, pick the line item and enter an amount.
3. Choose the source: one of your logged stashes, stock you have but haven't logged, or a pledge to gather it.
4. Click **Contribute**. A pledge shows as gathering until you log the stock.
5. If a line sets a minimum quality, a lot below it is logged but doesn't count.
6. Find your pledges under **My contributions**. Click **Withdraw** to return an amount to your free stock.

## {orange}Deliver your pledge to the drop-off{/orange}

Do this when a goal has a drop-off where the org collects the goods.

1. Open the goal and find **My contributions**.
2. Click **Plan my delivery**. The Cargo Planner opens with a pickup row for each stocked pledge.
3. Pick a location in any row with an empty from box.
4. Click **Plan route** and fly the run.
5. When the run ends, click **Mark delivered + move my holdings** so your inventory matches.

If you deliver without a run, click **Mark delivered** next to the contribution instead.

## {orange}Plan a pickup or sell run{/orange}

Do this to collect stock spread across several stations.

1. On **My holdings**, tick the box beside each holding to move.
2. In the bar that appears, type where to deliver, or pick a goal's drop-off.
3. Click **Plan pickup run** to haul them there, or **Sell at best price** to sell each commodity where it pays best.
4. In the Cargo Planner, click **Plan route**. In sell mode, choose best aUEC (in-game currency) per hour or most aUEC first.
5. Fly the run. At the end, accept the offer to update your inventory.

## {orange}Check what you can craft{/orange}

1. Open the **Blueprints** tab. It starts on **My library**.
2. Search for a recipe you own and click **Add**.
3. Read the Craftable column: how many times your free stock covers the recipe, and at what quality. The weakest input sets it.
4. Click **Gather** to start a goal for the materials, or **List** to post the crafted item on the Marketplace.
5. Switch to **Org readiness** to see how many members hold each recipe.

## {orange}Tips{/orange}

- Pick locations from the suggestions. A name the app doesn't recognise leaves an empty from box in pickup runs.
- Once part of a lot is pledged, its quality is locked. Use **Split** to re-rate it.
