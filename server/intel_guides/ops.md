## {orange}At a glance{/orange}

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who** | Everyone on a mission. Leaders and their deputies run it. |
| **What** | The live page for one mission, then its permanent record: attendance, money and loot. |
| **Where** | The Rally the Org group on the launcher, at `#/ops`. Each op has its own page at `#/ops/<id>`. |
| **When** | During a mission and right after it, while the money gets settled. |
| **Why** | Everyone sees who was there, who gets what and who won each drop. |

## {orange}The basics{/orange}

An op moves through four phases, shown as a track at the top of its page: Setup, Live, Settle and Closed. Each phase shows only the tools for that job.

An op starts one of two ways. A leader clicks Run mission on an Event Planner event, and the signups land on the roster. Or someone starts a quick op from the Ops home for an unplanned run.

The Ops home lists what's running now, what's settling up, any payments waiting on you, and past records.

Every change goes in the op's log with who made it and when. A mistake is voided, not deleted, so it stays on the record struck through.

How money and loot are shared is set by the op's payout rules. Your org decides those rules. Contracts are listed apart from the split: the game pays each contract in equal cuts to everyone who has it shared.

## {orange}Join a running op{/orange}

Do this when you show up for a mission.

1. Open `#/ops` and click the op under Running now, or follow the link from the event page.
2. If you're not on the roster, use the button at the top of the op that adds you as a walk-in.
3. Wait for the leader to mark you. Only they can set your attendance.
4. For each contract, click **I have it** once it's shared with you.

## {orange}Log money and settle your cut{/orange}

Do this when you sell loot, earn a bonus or pay a cost during the op. aUEC is the in-game currency.

1. In the Payout section, pick Income, Bonus or Expense.
2. Enter the amount, who is holding or paid it, and a note, then click **Log it**.
3. After the mission ends, open the split. It shows your cut and whether you send or receive.
4. If you send: copy the amount, send it in-game, then click **Mark sent**.
5. If you receive: click **Confirm received** when the money lands.
6. If only part arrived, click **Dispute…** and enter how much did. Click **Rest arrived** when the remainder comes in.

Only the person receiving a payment can confirm it.

## {orange}Roll for loot{/orange}

Do this when the leader adds a drop to the Loot panel.

1. Find the item in the Loot panel.
2. On your own row, pick Need, Want or Pass. Everyone can see every answer.
3. Wait for the leader to click **Roll**. Answers lock and the winner is shown.
4. To check the rolls were fair, click the Verify button. Your browser recomputes them.

If you don't answer, you count as Want. You can't roll until the leader has marked your attendance.

## {orange}Run an op as a leader{/orange}

Do this when you're leading the mission.

1. Start from the event with **Run mission**, or on the Ops home type a name under Quick op and click **Start quick op**.
2. In Setup, add anyone missing from the roster: org members by name, or a guest who isn't in the org with **Add guest**. Add co-leads under **Deputies**.
3. Check the payout rules and contracts, then click **Start mission**. Rule changes after this need a reason and everyone sees them.
4. Mark each person Present, Late, Left early, Excused or Absent as they arrive.
5. Click **End mission** when the op is over.
6. In Settle, mark anyone still unmarked. An unmarked person gets no share.
7. Click **Close the record**. Open payments can still be settled after closing.

## {orange}Tips{/orange}

- Mark people as they arrive. Late and Left early stamp the time only while the mission is live.
- If someone signed up but didn't come, mark them Absent rather than removing them.
- Log money under the person who is actually holding it, so the payment list tells them who to pay.
- After the op, anyone on it can click **Write debrief** to start an after-action report in Org Intel.
